Blender hair demo
=================

Head scan
---------

bust.glb, the bust_*.jpg textures Godot extracts from it, the "bust", "head_collider" and
"body_collider" objects in source/blender_hair.blend and head_collider.tfxmesh and
body_collider.tfxmesh are "Infinite, 3D Head Scan" by
Lee Perry-Smith, Infinite-Realities (http://www.ir-ltd.net/), licensed under a Creative Commons
Attribution 3.0 Unported License (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). Based on a work
at www.triplegangers.com. Taken from the three.js repository
(https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js, examples/models/gltf/LeePerrySmith).

Changes: scaled to real size, UVs flipped vertically, colour and normal maps packed into the
files; the colliders are copies cut at the neck, closed and decimated.
